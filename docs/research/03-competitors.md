# Güd Vector competitive landscape

**Market:** Bay Area local service businesses  
**Offer:** a custom website plus a bespoke AI system for one specific business — quote drafting with owner approval, a customer portal (pay / pause / cancel), and scheduling. Not a generic SaaS the customer configures.  
**Researched:** 2 Sep 2026 (PT). Pricing year-stamped to 2026. Quotes are from vendor sites, review aggregators, or named 2026 summaries — not invented.

---

## How to read this map

Güd Vector sits in a hole most vendors refuse to occupy:

- **Local web agencies** sell a site (and maybe SEO). They rarely own quoting, owner-approval, payments, pause/cancel, and scheduling as one system.
- **Done-for-you “contractor website + CRM” shops** sell a productized HighLevel-style stack. Fast and cheap. Not built around one owner’s quote rules.
- **Field-service SaaS** (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, QuoteIQ) is self-serve or sales-led software the shop must configure. Websites are an afterthought.
- **AI receptionist tools** answer the phone. They do not draft owner-gated quotes, run a branded portal, or replace the website.

The attack is not “we have more features.” It is: **this is yours, it matches how you actually quote and serve customers, and the owner stays in the loop.**

### Direct set, in one sentence each

- **Tenaya360 / Sama Nova** are the Bay Area shops a contractor will actually be referred to. They win on local presence and SEO retainers. They do not ship the product Güd Vector ships.
- **Primate / Contractor Site Design / Webpage Conversion / Home Service Direct** are national contractor-web factories. Site in days or weeks. Quoting, portal, and scheduling are “embed Jobber” at best.
- **Novaica / LeadWise / Royalso** are the HighLevel done-for-you wave. Cheap monthly, cancel-and-lose-the-site, generic AI estimates, no owner gate.
- **DK Studio** is the custom-AI peer on price ($15k–$60k) with zero home-service distribution.

No named Bay Area firm was found that publicly sells *custom field service software* as a productized offer to trades. “Custom CRM for contractors” in the wild is almost always JobNimbus/AccuLynx configuration, a HighLevel snapshot, or a WordPress form. That absence is the opening.

## 4. Positioning gaps Güd Vector should exploit

Each gap is tied to a sourced competitor weakness. Use these as talk tracks, not as feature lists.

### Gap 1 — Owner-gated quoting, not customer self-serve and not “send whatever the template spits out”

- **Jobber** has quoting and even “Draft for me,” but Service Business Academy (2026) is blunt: **no native customer self-quoting**; the workaround is ResponsiBid at **$179–$229/mo plus $400–$600 setup**.
- **QuoteIQ** goes the other way: InstaQuote is the *customer* pricing the job. That is a different product. Elite/Max gating of InstaSchedule also means cheap tiers cannot even let a customer book.
- **Housecall Pro** users report the estimate/schedule objects are rigid (Software Connect: extending a one-day job means opening a new project).
- **EstimatorSuite 2026:** 47% of contractors still quote in sheets/paper *because they do not trust software with the number*. Bid speed (under 24 hours) is what actually lifts win rate (**+31%**).

**Talk track:** “The AI drafts it in *your* structure. It does not go to the customer until you approve it. That is why owners who refused Jobber/QuoteIQ will buy this.”

### Gap 2 — A real website they own, not a CRM subdomain

- Kore Komfort (Mar 2026) and Untap Web: Jobber (and HCP) builders have **no blog, no city pages, no schema, no ownership**. Cancel the CRM, lose the site.
- Novaica’s own FAQ: cancel and “You lose access to the website and the systems.”
- Tenaya360 and Home Service Direct already charge **$5k–$15k** for the site *alone* and then bolt on a form.

**Talk track:** “Jobber will give you a free site that cannot rank. Novaica will rent you a 20-page template. We build the site *and* the system behind it, and it is yours.”

Do not pretend to beat Tenaya360 at 12-month local SEO retainers. Partner or stay silent on “we’ll get you to #1.”

### Gap 3 — One business’s rules, not a one-size pricebook

- ServiceTitan: per-tech pricing, 12–16 week (or longer) implementation, contracts, a pricebook designed for 20-truck shops (Construction Perks / Full Stack HVAC 2026).
- JobNimbus official FAQ: they hide pricing *because* “contracting businesses aren’t one-size-fits-all” — then they still ship four generic tiers with automation and integration caps (10 automations / 2 integrations on Essentials).
- Launch27 is excellent at beds/baths math and useless the moment the business is not cleaning.
- EstimatorSuite: electricians and landscapers are called out as **open market** for industry-specific gaps; landscapers have the **lowest** software adoption (31%).

**Talk track:** “Your pause policy, your cancel window, your trip fee, your ‘we don’t quote roofs over the phone’ rule — those are code, not a settings checkbox you will never find.”

### Gap 4 — Customer portal for pay / pause / cancel, not a ‘Client Hub’ bolted onto invoices

- Jobber Client Hub: approve quotes, see appointments, pay invoices. That is pay-the-invoice, not **pause or cancel the service** the way a membership/recurring or project-based local business actually works.
- QuoteIQ: client portal starts at **Pro $149.99**; booking link only at **$299+**.
- Square Appointments / Wix / Calendly: cancel/reschedule a 30-minute slot. They do not hold a project, a seasonal contract, or an owner-approved change order.
- Launch27 assumes cleaning recurrence and still makes you bring your own website (Bizzly 2026).

**Talk track:** “Homeowners already expect Uber-like pause/cancel. Your SaaS gives them a pay link. We give them *your* portal with *your* rules.”

### Gap 5 — Setup is the product, not a 14-day trial the owner abandons

- ServiceTitan: months and $5k–$50k before value (multiple 2026 reviews). BBB stories of paying a year while still not live.
- JobNimbus: weeks of workflow building; Professional Services extra.
- EstimatorSuite: **28%** of non-adopters cite learning curve; **22%** cite too many options; **58%** of “satisfied” software users still have a broken critical feature.
- DK Studio prices the honest version of custom AI at **$15k–$60k** and 4–8 weeks — with no website and no trade specialization.

**Talk track:** “You are not configuring our app. We are encoding how you already work. That is why this is a build, not a seat license.”

### Gap 6 — An agent that works *your* job, not a generic receptionist meter

- Smith.ai official: **$300 for 30 human calls**, booking extra **$1.50**; overages **$8.50–$11.50**. Fine for a law firm; brutal for a buzzing HVAC week.
- Ruby: **$720 for 200 minutes**.
- Goodcall/Rosie/Podium AI Employee: answer and maybe book. Scripts are generic; Goodcall had **zero** G2/Capterra/Trustpilot reviews as of Apr 2026.
- Jobber Receptionist is an add-on (**$29** on Jobber’s own 2 Sep 2026 pricing page; some 2026 blogs still quote $99 — confirm live before citing a dollar in a pitch). It books into Jobber. It does not become the business’s quote brain.

**Talk track:** “Those tools answer the phone. Ours drafts the quote, waits for you, then lets the customer pay, pause, or cancel in a portal that looks like your company — not like Smith.ai.”

## 5. Who Güd Vector should not pick a fight with

Be explicit in sales conversations. Credibility comes from conceding the right hills.

| Incumbent | Let them have |
|---|---|
| **ServiceTitan** | 20–200 tech shops, pricebook discipline, dispatch, marketing attribution, financing ecosystem. |
| **Jobber** | 1–10 person crews that want to be live this week on scheduling + invoicing + a Client Hub. |
| **Housecall Pro** | Residential close-rate via visual proposals and the 200k-pro peer group. |
| **Square** | Payments rails, hardware, Tap to Pay. Use them; do not rebuild them. |
| **Tenaya360 / Sama Nova** | Ongoing Bay Area SEO retainers and “we’ll answer Jack’s call.” Compete on the *system*, not on who posts more city pages. |
| **Ruby** | The rare high-ticket first impression that must be a warm human every time. |

The winnable account looks like: **owner-operator or <15 people, Bay Area, distinctive quoting rules, recurring or project work that needs pause/cancel, embarrassed by the Facebook page, allergic to a 12-month ServiceTitan contract, unwilling to let a bot send a number they have not seen.**
